# Art Direction Guidelines: Lead Edge Marketing

## 1. Aesthetic DNA
- **Mood:** High-craft, cinematic, warm architectural, tactile studio environment.
- **Lighting:** Soft daylight studio illumination with directional key light; warm tungsten and champagne rim highlights echoing the brand's brushed gold metallic logo.
- **Color Grading:** Monochromatic neutral foundations with warm off-whites, charcoal slates, deep blacks, and burnished bronze accents.
- **NO Blue Rule:** Absolute prohibition of blue lighting, cyan glows, blue sky backgrounds, or blue UI elements in any creative asset or mockups.

## 2. Hero Visual Composition (The 4 Layers)
The homepage hero features a 4-layer editorial stack representing Lead Edge Marketing's 4 core marketing disciplines:
1. **Layer 1: Web Development & Digital Architecture**
   - High-craft browser / editorial digital canvas, showing precise typography, responsive layout geometry, and warm neutral device mockups.
2. **Layer 2: Social Media Content & Creative Direction**
   - Editorial photography, tactile creative direction, typography, cinematic model frame in warm studio setting.
3. **Layer 3: Meta Advertising & Paid Campaign Analytics**
   - High-fidelity performance dashboard in brand colors (charcoal dark mode, warm bronze metrics, titanium gray gridlines, zero blue).
4. **Layer 4: Search Engine Optimization & Technical Visibility**
   - Precision search visibility matrix, organic growth curves in gold gradient, indexing geometry, clean architectural motif.

## 3. 3D Smartphone Scene (/about)
- **Device Design:** Custom unbranded titanium flagship smartphone with physically realistic 19.5:9 proportions, satin titanium outer frame, ceramic back, micro-beveled glass screen.
- **Screen Asset:** High-resolution vertical social video reel with custom generic UI (avatar, engagement ticker, soundwave, caption) in brand colors.
- **Lighting:** Studio HDRI reflections with gentle rim light along the chamfered metallic edges.
- **Zero Trademarks:** No Apple or Samsung logos, icons, or proprietary notch/dynamic-island designs.

## 4. Asset Generation & Curation Rules
- No deformed anatomy, garbled text, or AI collage artifacts.
- All interface copy and typography must be rendered cleanly via SVG or HTML overlay rather than blurry AI text.
- Formats: Modern WebP / AVIF with PNG fallback, explicit aspect ratios (`16/10`, `4/3`, `9/16`), and responsive sizes.

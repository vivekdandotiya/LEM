SEO FAQ Section:
1. What do SEO services include?
Answer: Lead Edge Marketers provides technical SEO audits, keyword research, on-page optimization, backlink building, and local SEO services. We structure your site to earn high search rankings and drive consistent organic traffic.

2. How long does SEO take to work?
Answer: Professional SEO is a long-term campaign that typically delivers noticeable keyword ranking gains and traffic growth within 3 to 6 months. Initial technical site fixes provide early traction, but lasting search engine authority builds over time.

3. Which SEO metrics do you report on?
Answer: We track transparent, revenue-driving KPIs including organic keyword rankings, monthly organic traffic growth, domain authority, click-through rates (CTR), conversion rates, and total leads generated. You receive detailed monthly reports demonstrating clear campaign ROI.

4. What is the difference between an SEO agency and an SEO consultant?
Answer: An SEO consultant primarily provides strategic advice, site audits, and recommendations for your internal team to execute. Lead Edge Marketers operates as a full-service SEO agency, handling strategy, technical fixes, content creation, and ongoing optimization from start to finish.

PPC FAQ Section:
1. What do Facebook marketing services include?
Answer: Lead Edge Marketers handles custom ad creation, audience targeting, campaign setup, continuous A/B testing, and conversion tracking to ensure your Meta ads generate consistent leads and sales.

2. How long does Facebook marketing take to show results?
Answer: Meta ads start generating website traffic and lead inquiries almost immediately after launching, with full campaign optimization and steady return on ad spend achieved within the first 2 to 4 weeks.

3. How much does a Facebook marketing agency cost?
Answer: Agency fees depend on your total monthly ad spend and project goals, with Lead Edge Marketers offering flexible performance retainers designed to maximize your profit and return on investment.

4. Should I use Advantage+ or manual targeting on Facebook?
Answer: Advantage+ uses Meta's AI algorithms to reach broad potential buyers fast, while manual targeting lets us focus on specific niche demographics; we often test both to find what delivers the lowest cost per lead.

5. Are Facebook Groups worth it for a small business?
Answer: Yes, running or participating in active Facebook Groups helps build direct community trust, though combining group engagement with targeted paid ads yields much faster lead generation.
